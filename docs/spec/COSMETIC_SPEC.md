# Cosmetic and Prestige Specification

Status legend: **DECIDED** = agreed product rule; **TBD** = intentionally unresolved.

## DECIDED — No gameplay power
All crowns, backgrounds/themes, reveal effects and prestige cosmetics are cosmetic only. Neither Honor Crowns nor purchasable crowns provide gameplay/stat bonuses.

## DECIDED — Crown categories
### Honor Crown
Prestige-only crown awarded through achievements such as:
- seasons;
- rankings;
- challenges;
- special events;
- other high-prestige accomplishments.

Honor Crowns are **not for sale**. Their purpose is to visibly communicate accomplishment.

### Purchasable / value crown
Cosmetic crown available through the store using QC. It exists for visual customization, not competitive power.

## DECIDED — Board/background themes
Themes/backgrounds are permanent cosmetic collectibles and may be sold for QC or awarded through approved progression/events.

## DECIDED — Crown reveal effects
Players can equip an effect that plays when a queen is found/revealed. Planned examples include:
- fireworks;
- flashing/sparkle;
- black hole;
- future visual effects.

Reveal effects are permanent cosmetics and may be sold for QC. Equipped effects may also play when a queen is revealed through an assist item because the effect itself provides no gameplay advantage.

## DECIDED — Titles and prestige
Titles are a visible prestige/reward system, particularly suitable for:
- Perfect Streak milestones;
- special challenges;
- seasons/ranking achievements.

Titles may be displayed with player identity in competitive/social surfaces such as leaderboards.

## DECIDED — Cosmetic loadout direction
The architecture should support an equipped cosmetic loadout containing at least:
- crown;
- board/background theme;
- crown reveal effect;
- title.
